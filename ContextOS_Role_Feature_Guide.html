ContextOS · Role and feature guide

What ContextOS does for each role

ContextOS helps a company onboard people and keep its knowledge findable. It reads your connected sources, builds a cited Knowledge Hub, answers questions through Context Bot, and runs onboarding from first day to the end of probation. This guide walks through the app as each role sees it, says what works today, and lists what is still missing.

Roles at a glance

The spec describes four roles. The app has six: it adds Owner (the admin who created the workspace) and Guest (a time-limited outsider). Each person has exactly one role per workspace. The six roles share four experiences: Owner and Admin see the admin app, Member and Guest see the employee app.

EM

Member (Employee)

Employee experience

Follows their onboarding roadmap, learns, finds knowledge and asks Context Bot.
GU

Guest

Employee experience, limited

Contractor or partner with an end date who sees only the projects they're added to.
MG

Manager

Manager experience

Supports their team's onboarding, runs check-ins and recommends probation outcomes.
HR

HR (People Ops)

People Ops experience

Runs onboarding across the company, manages templates and records probation decisions.
AD

Admin

Admin experience

Manages members and roles, connectors, access policies, sign-in, AI settings and the audit log.
OW

Owner

Admin experience, plus ownership

Created the workspace. Has everything an admin has, plus ownership and workspace deletion.

What every role gets

These features are the same for everyone. What a person sees inside them is filtered by what they could open in the original source, such as a Google Drive file. Admins don't get around this filter.

Context Bot and knowledge

  • Works
    Ask Context Bot from any page

    Button in the rail, floating button on phones, or ⌘J. Suggested questions change by role. Inside a project, conversations are saved privately and answers stream in with citations.

  • Works
    Answers only from sources you can open

    Google Drive sharing is copied onto every file and passage. Access policies can hide a source or keep it out of answers.

  • Works
    Answers follow the admin's AI settings

    Strictness, citing sources and the stale-source warning shape every answer. A question many people ask without an answer goes to its knowledge owners.

  • Works
    Pay, bonus, performance and disciplinary questions are refused

    Same for every role. The bot points the person to their People partner.

  • Works
    Rate an answer, report a knowledge gap

    Helpful or not helpful with a reason. A reported gap goes to the domain owners of the project it's most likely about, and to admins only when nobody owns it.

  • Works
    Knowledge Hub

    Search, saved items, "Start here" and seven collection chips. Each project is filed under Company, Products, Projects or Processes when it's created, and can be moved later.

  • Works
    Project hub

    Cited overview, decisions, current state, people, systems, terms, timeline and sources. Each claim links to its evidence. Items can be flagged as incorrect, outdated or conflicting. Owners, admins and managers rename a project from its header; owners and admins delete it.

  • Works
    Confirm, mark stale or dispute an item

    Shown only to the project's or the workspace's domain owners, who admins appoint on Members & roles.

  • Works
    Search

    The top-bar search opens a results page with knowledge items, sources, projects and people, type filters and "Ask Context Bot".

People, settings, notifications

  • Works
    People directory and profiles

    Search (including skills), filters (my team, experts, new), teams view, reporting line, "Ask an expert". Profiles show pronouns, bio and skills. Not for guests.

  • Placeholder
    Message someone, book time

    The buttons on profiles, Home cards, item pages and bot answers only say "coming soon". The onboarding record's Message is the exception: it sends an in-app message.

  • Works
    Settings · Profile

    Name, time zone, pronouns, location, bio, skills and avatar shape. Job title and team are shown read-only, from the HR system.

  • Partly works
    Settings · Notifications

    In-app and email switches per event, quiet hours. Email arrives as one summary per person, security alerts straight away. Slack isn't built.

  • Works
    Settings · Preferences

    Theme, reduce motion and the Context Bot options.

  • Works
    Settings · Security

    Sign-in method, devices (sign out one or all others), connected apps and "Request a copy of your data".

  • Works
    Notifications

    Bell menu with the latest 8, and a full page with the latest 100, an unread filter, mark read and mark all read.

  • Works
    Switch workspace, sign out

    From the account menu, when the person belongs to more than one workspace.

EM
Role · member

Member (Employee)

Any employee, including new hires. Their job in ContextOS: follow their onboarding roadmap, learn, find knowledge and ask Context Bot.

Nav: Home · My roadmap · Knowledge Hub · PeopleData scope: own records, shared knowledge
The employee screens now come from the server: Home, the roadmap, modules, lessons, knowledge checks and the first-day welcome, and progress is saved. Contributions is the only screen still on sample data, and it's hidden.

Features

  • Works
    First-day welcome

    Three steps: who's supporting you (manager, buddy, People partner), set up your profile, your first 90 days. Opens on a new hire's first visit each session until they finish it.

  • Works
    Home

    Up next (resume a module), your onboarding progress, this week's tasks (to do, overdue, done), milestones, check-ins, ask band, knowledge updates for you, your people.

  • Works
    My roadmap

    Stages, modules and status, progress, completed list. "Request a change" goes to the manager and "Request clarification" to the module owner.

  • Works
    Module and lesson pages

    Lessons, outcomes, sources, people, rating. Select text in a lesson to ask Context Bot about it. Mark complete and continue.

  • Works
    Request access, open source, open decision record, play video

    "Request access" on restricted lessons goes to admins, without showing the person titles they can't open. Buttons with nothing to open are hidden.

  • Works
    Knowledge check

    Multiple choice, submit, see explanations, retake. The server pauses Context Bot while the check is open.

  • Works
    See learning a manager assigned

    On the roadmap and in "This week", marked "Assigned by". Needs an onboarding record.

  • Works
    See upcoming and shared check-ins

    A Check-ins card on Home with upcoming dates and the notes the manager shared, never ratings. Replying from the app isn't built yet.

  • Preview data
    Contributions

    Write knowledge, draft from sources, pick a reviewer. Hidden for the 18 Oct demo, and the server doesn't provide it yet.

  • Works
    Propose a bot answer for the Knowledge Hub

    From any answer in a project. It goes to the Review queue.

  • Works
    Request a different role

    From Settings: member, manager, HR or admin, with a reason. One open request at a time.

  • Works
    Knowledge Hub, People, Context Bot, Settings

    As described under "What every role gets".

  • Server only
    Kudos, badges, leaderboards, rewards, bounties

    Built on the server for a later phase. No screens.

What's missing for members

  1. Server support for Contributions. Hidden until the team decides whether it comes back after the demo.
  2. Messaging and booking time with people.
  3. Replying to a shared check-in from the app.
GU
Role · guest

Guest

A contractor, agency or partner who needs a narrow, time-limited view. This role is not in the spec. Guests see only the projects they've been added to, on a Home of their own.

Nav: Home · Knowledge HubData scope: member projects onlyAccess ends: within 180 days

Features

  • Works
    Invited with an end date and projects

    An owner or admin invites a guest, picks the projects they may see and sets an access end date within 180 days.

  • Works
    Change a guest's projects

    From the guest's row on Members & roles. Both changes are audited.

  • Works
    Access ends automatically

    After the end date the guest can no longer sign in to the workspace.

  • Works
    Guest Home

    Their projects, when their access ends, and Context Bot. No onboarding roadmap.

  • Works
    Knowledge Hub and Context Bot

    Limited to their projects and to sources they can open.

  • Works
    Kept out of the directory

    No People page, no member list, no person facets in search.

  • Works
    Kept out of contributing

    Can't propose answers to the Hub, request a role, connect apps or give kudos.

What's missing for guests

  1. A decision in the spec on whether guests exist and what they may see.
MG
Role · manager

Manager

People managers. They track and support their own team's onboarding, run check-ins and recommend probation outcomes. Team means direct reports, or the whole tree when the workspace setting includes indirect reports. Every manager page follows the same setting.

Nav: Home · Team onboarding · Knowledge Hub · Review queue · People · My learningData scope: own + team
Every manager screen is backed by the server. What's left is shared with other roles: messaging, and Slack delivery.

Features

  • Works
    Home

    Needs your attention (blocked, at risk, overdue), upcoming check-ins with "Summarize", team roster, knowledge gaps on your team, team knowledge. Quick "Assign learning" and "Record check-in" for whoever needs it most.

  • Works
    Team onboarding

    A card per team member with stage, progress and status, plus knowledge-check results by topic across the team.

  • Works
    Member page

    Progress against plan, editable current stage of the roadmap, check results, what's coming up, check-in history, probation section and an AI insight with suggested actions.

  • Works
    Assign learning or a whole roadmap

    Pick a team member, a module, task or check, a due date and a note. Or assign a roadmap from a template with a buddy. The person sees it on their roadmap.

  • Works
    Record a check-in

    Weekly 1:1, 30-day review, 60-day readiness or probation recommendation. Pulse, ratings, blockers, notes, next steps, and an option to share the notes with the person.

  • Works
    Recommend a probation outcome

    A probation check-in sends the recommendation to HR as "Ready for approval".

  • Works
    Escalate a blocker

    Access problems go to admins, everything else to HR.

  • Works
    Knowledge gaps on your team

    Questions the team asked without a good answer, shown only above a minimum count so no one is singled out. "Request update" flags the source.

  • Works
    Review queue

    Approve or request changes on bot answers people proposed for the Hub. The reviewer is the project's domain owner, otherwise the author's manager.

  • Works
    My learning

    Assigned, suggested (with Add) and completed courses. Every course listed opens.

  • Works
    Create projects and manage sources

    Add or paste sources, import or sync from Google Drive, reprocess, delete. Rename a project from its header.

  • Works
    Weekly team digest

    Sent when the admin turns it on, in the app and by email.

  • Works
    Invite someone to your team

    "Invite to my team" on Team onboarding. They join as a Member who reports to you.

  • Works
    Make a report a mentor or path curator

    "Mentor or curator" on a report's page.

  • Works
    Build learning paths

    Tutorials are the app's learning paths. A manager who is a path curator creates, AI-drafts, publishes and assigns them in Tutorial Studio.

  • Placeholder
    Message a team member

    From Home and the attention list.

What's missing for managers

  1. Messaging team members.
  2. Slack delivery for the weekly digest and notifications.
HR
Role · hr

HR (People Ops)

People partners. They run onboarding across the company, manage onboarding templates and record employment status decisions. HR is its own role, so an HR person can't also be a manager or admin.

Nav: Home · Onboarding · Probation reviews · Templates · People · Knowledge Hub · My learning · Audit log · TutorialsData scope: whole organisation, onboarding only

Features

  • Works
    Home

    Active onboardings, at risk or blocked, probation reviews in the next 30 days, people starting in the next 14 days, onboarding by team, overdue milestones with "Follow up", templates in use.

  • Works
    Onboarding list and "New onboarding"

    Everyone onboarding, with search and filters. New onboarding takes name, job title, email, team, start date, manager, buddy and template, and sends the invitation.

  • Works
    Onboarding record

    Change manager, buddy, template or People partner. Setup checklist, progress and milestones. Message sends an in-app message to the new hire, manager or buddy.

  • Works
    Setup checklist

    "Account active" ticks itself when the person first signs in. Laptop, calendar and the other steps are ticked by hand.

  • Works
    Probation reviews

    Filters (all, needs you, awaiting manager, at risk, completed) and reminders to managers in bulk.

  • Works
    Record the employment decision

    Read the manager's recommendation, milestones and activity, then confirm employment, extend probation, or ask the manager to discuss first. Written to the audit log.

  • Works
    Onboarding templates

    New template (copied from an existing one), modules by stage, reorder, add from library, audience, save draft, publish a new version, version history. Archive, delete and reopen, and open published versions. Draft saves are audited.

  • Works
    Onboarding card on people's profiles

    For anyone currently onboarding, with a link to the record.

  • Works
    Edit people's job details

    Job title, team and manager, from their profile. Not their own. Roles stay with admins.

  • Works
    Audit log of People Ops events

    Check-ins, recommendations, decisions and template changes, with their details.

  • Works
    Invited directly as HR

    Admins can invite someone as HR, with job title, team, manager and start date.

  • Works
    My learning and Tutorials

    HR's own courses, and Tutorial Studio to build and assign learning content.

What's missing for HR

  1. Automatic setup steps and job details once directory sync (SCIM) exists.
  2. Messaging from places other than the onboarding record.
AD
Role · admin

Admin

IT and workspace admins. They govern the workspace: members and roles, connected sources, who can see what, sign-in, AI behaviour and the audit trail. They don't see onboarding records or check-in notes.

Nav: Overview · Tutorials · Members & roles · Connectors · Access policies · AI settings · Audit log · Workspace · Knowledge Hub · Workspace setupData scope: whole workspace, no onboarding data

Features

  • Works
    Workspace overview

    Connector health with Reconnect, access and security checks, sync activity, AI answer quality with quick switches, latest audit events, member counts.

  • Works
    Members & roles

    Search and filter, change a role (never your own, never to Owner), suspend, reactivate or offboard. Offboarding removes access and creates a hand-over checklist for the manager.

  • Works
    Invite people, see pending invites, approve role requests

    Invite by email as Member, Manager, HR, Admin or Guest. A Pending invitations card lists unanswered invites with Revoke. Approve or decline role requests, with an extra confirmation for Admin.

  • Works
    Grant responsibilities

    A Responsibilities tab lists who owns what, grants domain owner, path curator or mentor, and removes them.

  • Works
    Departments and teams

    Create, rename, nest and delete.

  • Not built
    Directory sync (SCIM)

    "Sync now" records an empty run. Names, titles, teams and managers don't come from the HR system.

  • Works
    Connect Google Drive

    Authorize, choose folders or shared drives, share with everyone or not, pick a project, sync frequency and age cut-off. Manage, sync now, reconnect.

  • Not built
    Every other connector

    Slack, Jira, GitHub, OneDrive, Teams, Confluence, Notion and others are listed but answer "isn't available yet". "Request a connector" works.

  • Works
    Access policies

    Source permissions are always mirrored. Add Restrict (hide) or Exclude (never used in answers) policies by team or department, resolve conflicts, preview what one person can access.

  • Works
    Share one source with one person

    Share on a pasted or uploaded source lists who can open it, adds someone with an optional end date, and removes them. Connected sources follow their provider.

  • Works
    Single sign-on

    Add an OIDC or SAML connection, verify the domain with a DNS record, require SSO.

  • Works
    AI settings

    Strictness, citing sources, the stale-source warning, routing unanswered questions to owners and the pause during knowledge checks all take effect. The weekly digest goes to managers in the app and by email.

  • Works
    Audit log

    Search, date range, category filters, expand for details, and Export CSV for the chosen range. People Ops details are left out.

  • Works
    Workspace page

    Download the workspace's data (no people data or conversations) and aggregate analytics for 30 or 90 days, with rates hidden below the privacy threshold.

  • Works
    Workspace setup checklist

    Connect a source, choose content, create a project, invite your team, start a new hire's onboarding.

  • Works
    Tutorials

    Create, AI-draft, publish and assign learning content in Tutorial Studio.

  • Server only
    Rewards catalog, points adjustments, anomaly review

    Later phase. No screens.

What's missing for admins

  1. Connectors beyond Google Drive. Slack and Jira first.
  2. Directory sync (SCIM), or hiding "Sync now" until it exists.
  3. A People item in the admin navigation, if admins should browse the directory.
OW
Role · owner

Owner

The person who created the workspace, plus anyone an owner promotes. Not in the spec. Owners see the same admin app, with a few extra powers that protect the workspace itself.

Nav: same as AdminData scope: whole workspace, no onboarding data

Features

  • Works
    Everything an admin can do

    See the Admin section.

  • Works
    Create a workspace

    Company name and address. The creator becomes Owner.

  • Works
    Make someone an Owner, or remove ownership

    Only owners can. The last owner can't be removed.

  • Works
    Can always sign in when SSO is required

    So a broken SSO setup can't lock the workspace out.

  • Works
    Schedule or cancel workspace deletion

    On the Workspace page: type the workspace's slug to schedule deletion in 7 days, and cancel any time before.

  • Server only
    Run performance review cycles

    Part of a later add-on. No screens.

What's missing for owners

  1. The Owner role in the spec, so the team agrees on what only owners may do.

Across roles

Open decisions and what the app does today

The ten open questions from section 8 of the role access spec, with the behaviour the code has settled on so far. Questions 5, 6 and 8 are now decided.

#QuestionWhat the app does today
1Can Admin see onboarding progress?No. All People Ops pages refuse admins, and the audit log, its CSV and the workspace export leave out People Ops details.
2Who changes probation to full-time?The manager recommends and HR decides. Matches the prototype.
3Can a manager edit or remove team members?No. Managers invite members into their own team and make reports mentors or path curators, but can't edit or remove people.
4Can HR add, edit and remove people and change roles?HR creates onboarding records, which send the invite, and edits people's job title, team and manager from their profile (not their own). Only admins change roles.
5Does HR get an audit log page?Decided: yes, an audit log of People Ops events.
6Do HR and Admin get their own learning roadmap?Decided: HR has My learning. Admins have none, but build tutorials.
7Can employees publish knowledge directly?No. Proposed answers go to the Review queue.
8Manager scope: direct reports or the whole tree?Decided: the workspace setting, everywhere. Direct reports by default, the whole tree when indirect reports are on.
9One role per user, or several?One. An HR person can't also be a manager.
10Hide the manager Review queue for the demo?Still visible. It holds bot answers proposed for the Hub, not only contributions.
