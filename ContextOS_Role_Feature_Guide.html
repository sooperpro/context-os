ContextOS · Role and feature guide

What ContextOS does for each role

ContextOS helps a company onboard people and keep its knowledge findable. It reads your connected sources, builds a cited Knowledge Hub, answers questions through Context Bot, and runs onboarding from first day to the end of probation. This guide walks through the app as each role sees it, says what works today, and lists what is still missing.

Overview

Roles at a glance

The spec describes four roles. The app has six: it adds Owner (the admin who created the workspace) and Guest (a time-limited outsider). Each person has exactly one role per workspace. The six roles share four experiences: Owner and Admin see the admin app, Member and Guest see the employee app.

EM

Member (Employee)

Employee experience

Follows their onboarding roadmap, learns, finds knowledge and asks Context Bot.
GU

Guest

Employee experience, limited

Contractor or partner with an end date who sees only the projects they're added to.
MG

Manager

Manager experience

Supports their team's onboarding, runs check-ins and recommends probation outcomes.
HR

HR (People Ops)

People Ops experience

Runs onboarding across the company, manages templates and records probation decisions.
AD

Admin

Admin experience

Manages members and roles, connectors, access policies, sign-in, AI settings and the audit log.
OW

Owner

Admin experience, plus ownership

Created the workspace. Has everything an admin has, plus ownership and workspace deletion.

What every role gets

These features are the same for everyone. What a person sees inside them is filtered by what they could open in the original source, such as a Google Drive file. Admins don't get around this filter.

Context Bot and knowledge

  • Works
    Ask Context Bot from any page

    Button in the rail, floating button on phones, or ⌘J. Suggested questions change by role. Inside a project, conversations are saved privately and answers stream in with citations.

  • Works
    Answers only from sources you can open

    Google Drive sharing is copied onto every file and passage. Access policies can hide a source or keep it out of answers.

  • Works
    Pay, bonus, performance and disciplinary questions are refused

    Same for every role. The bot points the person to their People partner.

  • Works
    Rate an answer, report a knowledge gap

    Helpful or not helpful with a reason. "Report a knowledge gap" notifies admins. Inside a project it raises a flag on the project.

  • Partly works
    Knowledge Hub

    Search, saved items, "Start here" and seven category chips. Projects have no stored category, so Company, Products and Processes are always empty and everything shows under Projects.

  • Works
    Project hub

    Cited overview, decisions, current state, people, systems, terms, timeline and sources. Each claim links to its evidence. Items can be flagged as incorrect, outdated or conflicting.

  • Partly works
    Confirm, mark stale or dispute an item

    Buttons show for everyone but only a "domain owner" can use them, and there is no screen to make someone a domain owner.

  • Partly works
    Search box in the top bar

    Opens Context Bot with the text. The server has a full search, but there is no results page.

People, settings, notifications

  • Partly works
    People directory and profiles

    Search, filters (my team, experts, new), teams view, reporting line, "Ask an expert". Not for guests. Skills, bio and pronouns aren't stored yet.

  • Placeholder
    Message someone, book time

    The buttons appear on profiles, Home cards and item pages, but only say "coming soon".

  • Partly works
    Settings · Profile

    Name and time zone can be edited. Pronouns, location, bio, skills and avatar from the spec are missing.

  • Partly works
    Settings · Notifications

    In-app and email switches per event, quiet hours. Only in-app notifications are delivered. Email and Slack are not sent.

  • Partly works
    Settings · Preferences

    Theme and reduce motion work. The Context Bot options use preview data.

  • Partly works
    Settings · Security

    Sign-in method, devices (sign out one or all others) and connected apps work. "Request a copy of your data" uses preview data.

  • Partly works
    Notifications

    Bell menu with the latest 8, mark read, mark all read. There is no full notifications page.

  • Works
    Switch workspace, sign out

    From the account menu, when the person belongs to more than one workspace.

EM
Role · member

Member (Employee)

Any employee, including new hires. Their job in ContextOS: follow their onboarding roadmap, learn, find knowledge and ask Context Bot.

Nav: Home · My roadmap · Knowledge Hub · PeopleData scope: own records, shared knowledge
Biggest gap in the product. The employee screens are fully designed and built, but the server doesn't provide their data yet. In development they show sample data. In production, the employee Home cards, roadmap, lessons, knowledge checks and first-day welcome show "could not be loaded".

Features

  • Preview data
    First-day welcome

    Three steps: who's supporting you (manager, buddy, People partner), set up your profile, your first 90 days. Not opened automatically on first sign-in.

  • Preview data
    Home

    Up next (resume a module), your onboarding progress, this week's tasks (to do, overdue, done), milestones, ask band, knowledge updates for you, your people.

  • Preview data
    My roadmap

    Stages, modules and status, progress, completed list. "Request a change" goes to the manager and "Request clarification" to the module owner.

  • Preview data
    Module and lesson pages

    Lessons, outcomes, sources, people, rating. Select text in a lesson to ask Context Bot about it. Mark complete and continue.

  • Placeholder
    Request access, open source, open decision record, play video

    Buttons inside modules and lessons that only say "coming soon".

  • Preview data
    Knowledge check

    Multiple choice, submit, see explanations, retake. Context Bot pauses during the check in the app, but the server doesn't enforce the pause.

  • Server only
    See learning a manager assigned

    The server stores assignments, but the employee roadmap and "This week" don't read them yet.

  • Not built
    See upcoming and shared check-ins

    Managers can share a check-in with the person, but there is no way for the employee to read it.

  • Preview data
    Contributions

    Write knowledge, draft from sources, pick a reviewer. Hidden for the 18 Oct demo, and the server doesn't provide it yet.

  • Works
    Propose a bot answer for the Knowledge Hub

    From any answer in a project. It goes to the Review queue.

  • Works
    Request a different role

    From Settings: member, manager, HR or admin, with a reason. One open request at a time.

  • Works
    Knowledge Hub, People, Context Bot, Settings

    As described under "What every role gets".

  • Server only
    Kudos, badges, leaderboards, rewards, bounties

    Built on the server for a later phase. No screens.

What's missing for members

  1. Server support for Home, roadmap, modules, lessons, checks and welcome. Until then the core employee experience fails in production.
  2. Assigned learning showing up on the roadmap and "This week".
  3. Welcome opening automatically on a new hire's first sign-in.
  4. Reading check-ins the manager shared, and seeing upcoming check-in dates.
  5. Requesting access to a restricted source.
  6. Messaging and booking time with people.
  7. Profile fields: pronouns, location, bio, skills, avatar.
  8. Requesting a copy of your own data.
GU
Role · guest

Guest

A contractor, agency or partner who needs a narrow, time-limited view. This role is not in the spec. Guests get the employee screens, limited to the projects they've been added to.

Nav: Home · My roadmap · Knowledge HubData scope: member projects onlyAccess ends: within 180 days

Features

  • Works
    Invited with an end date

    An owner or admin invites a guest. The server requires an access end date within 180 days.

  • Works
    Access ends automatically

    After the end date the guest can no longer sign in to the workspace.

  • Server only
    Added to specific projects

    A guest sees nothing until they're added to a project, and there's no screen to add them.

  • Works
    Knowledge Hub and Context Bot

    Limited to their projects and to sources they can open.

  • Preview data
    Home and My roadmap

    The same onboarding screens as a member, which rarely fit a guest.

  • Works
    Kept out of the directory

    No People page, no member list, no person facets in search.

  • Works
    Kept out of contributing

    Can't propose answers to the Hub, request a role, connect apps or give kudos.

What's missing for guests

  1. A screen for admins to add a guest to a project. Without it, a guest sees an empty workspace.
  2. A guest Home that fits the role, instead of an onboarding roadmap.
  3. A decision in the spec on whether guests exist and what they may see.
MG
Role · manager

Manager

People managers. They track and support their own team's onboarding, run check-ins and recommend probation outcomes. Team means direct reports. A workspace setting can include indirect reports.

Nav: Home · Team onboarding · Knowledge Hub · Review queue · People · My learningData scope: own + team
The manager experience is the most complete in the app. Almost every screen is backed by the server.

Features

  • Works
    Home

    Needs your attention (blocked, at risk, overdue), upcoming check-ins with "Summarize", team roster, knowledge gaps on your team, team knowledge. Quick "Assign learning" and "Record check-in" for whoever needs it most.

  • Works
    Team onboarding

    A card per team member with stage, progress and status, plus knowledge-check results by topic across the team.

  • Works
    Member page

    Progress against plan, editable current stage of the roadmap, check results, what's coming up, check-in history, probation section and an AI insight with suggested actions.

  • Works
    Assign learning or a whole roadmap

    Pick a team member, a module, task or check, a due date and a note. Or assign a roadmap from a template with a buddy.

  • Works
    Record a check-in

    Weekly 1:1, 30-day review, 60-day readiness or probation recommendation. Pulse, ratings, blockers, notes, next steps, and an option to share with the person.

  • Works
    Recommend a probation outcome

    A probation check-in sends the recommendation to HR as "Ready for approval".

  • Works
    Escalate a blocker

    Access problems go to admins, everything else to HR.

  • Works
    Knowledge gaps on your team

    Questions the team asked without a good answer, shown only above a minimum count so no one is singled out. "Request update" flags the source.

  • Works
    Review queue

    Approve or request changes on bot answers people proposed for the Hub. The reviewer is the project's domain owner, otherwise the author's manager.

  • Partly works
    My learning

    Assigned, suggested (with Add) and completed courses. "Continue" on a course is a placeholder.

  • Works
    Create projects and manage sources

    Add or paste sources, import or sync from Google Drive, reprocess, delete.

  • Partly works
    Weekly team digest

    Sent when the admin turns it on, as an in-app notification only.

  • Placeholder
    Message a team member

    From Home and the attention list.

  • Server only
    Invite someone to your team, make a report a mentor or path curator

    The server allows it. The Members page is read-only for managers.

  • Server only
    Build learning paths

    Create, AI-generate, publish and assign paths. No screens.

What's missing for managers

  1. Assigned learning reaching the employee's screens. Managers can assign, but the employee can't see it in production.
  2. Messaging team members.
  3. Screens to invite into their team and to build learning paths.
  4. A consistent rule for indirect reports. Some pages honour the workspace setting, others only count direct reports.
  5. Email delivery for the weekly digest.
HR
Role · hr

HR (People Ops)

People partners. They run onboarding across the company, manage onboarding templates and record employment status decisions. HR is its own role, so an HR person can't also be a manager or admin.

Nav: Home · Onboarding · Probation reviews · Templates · People · Knowledge HubData scope: whole organisation, onboarding only

Features

  • Works
    Home

    Active onboardings, at risk or blocked, probation reviews in the next 30 days, people starting in the next 14 days, onboarding by team, overdue milestones with "Follow up", templates in use.

  • Works
    Onboarding list and "New onboarding"

    Everyone onboarding, with search and filters. New onboarding takes name, job title, email, team, start date, manager, buddy and template, and sends the invitation.

  • Works
    Onboarding record

    Change manager, buddy, template or People partner. Setup checklist: Google account, laptop, roadmap, buddy, welcome message, first-week calendar. Progress and milestones.

  • Partly works
    Setup checklist is ticked by hand

    "Google account created" is meant to come from directory sync (SCIM), which isn't built.

  • Works
    Probation reviews

    Filters (all, needs you, awaiting manager, at risk, completed) and reminders to managers in bulk.

  • Works
    Record the employment decision

    Read the manager's recommendation, milestones and activity, then confirm employment, extend probation, or ask the manager to discuss first. Written to the audit log.

  • Partly works
    Onboarding templates

    New template (copied from an existing one), modules by stage, reorder, add from library, audience, save draft, publish a new version, version history. You can't archive, delete or open an old version, and draft saves aren't audited.

  • Works
    Onboarding card on people's profiles

    For anyone currently onboarding, with a link to the record.

  • Placeholder
    Message from the onboarding record

    Shows "coming soon".

  • Not built
    HR-relevant audit log

    The spec says HR can view HR events. The audit log is admin only, and the in-app role table still says HR can see it.

  • Not built
    Invite someone directly as HR

    The invite dialog offers Member, Manager, Admin and Guest. HR has to be set afterwards by changing the role.

  • Not built
    HR's own learning roadmap

    HR has no learning pages. Open decision 6.

What's missing for HR

  1. An audit view filtered to HR events.
  2. Automatic setup steps once directory sync (SCIM) exists.
  3. Template archive, delete and past-version view. Auditing of draft saves.
  4. HR as an invite option.
  5. Messaging new hires and managers.
  6. A clear rule on HR changing someone's manager. The onboarding record can do it, but HR can't edit org details anywhere else.
AD
Role · admin

Admin

IT and workspace admins. They govern the workspace: members and roles, connected sources, who can see what, sign-in, AI behaviour and the audit trail. They don't see onboarding records or check-in notes.

Nav: Overview · Members & roles · Connectors · Access policies · AI settings · Audit log · Knowledge Hub · Workspace setupData scope: whole workspace, no onboarding data

Features

  • Works
    Workspace overview

    Connector health with Reconnect, access and security checks, sync activity, AI answer quality with quick switches, latest audit events, member counts.

  • Works
    Members & roles

    Search and filter, change a role (never your own, never to Owner), suspend, reactivate or offboard. Offboarding removes access and creates a hand-over checklist for the manager.

  • Works
    Invite people and approve role requests

    Invite by email as Member, Manager, Admin or Guest. Approve or decline role requests, with an extra confirmation for Admin.

  • Works
    Departments and teams

    Create, rename, nest and delete.

  • Not built
    Directory sync (SCIM)

    "Sync now" records an empty run. Names, titles, teams and managers don't come from the HR system.

  • Works
    Connect Google Drive

    Authorize, choose folders or shared drives, share with everyone or not, pick a project, sync frequency and age cut-off. Manage, sync now, reconnect.

  • Not built
    Every other connector

    Slack, Jira, GitHub, OneDrive, Teams, Confluence, Notion and others are listed but answer "isn't available yet". "Request a connector" works.

  • Works
    Access policies

    Source permissions are always mirrored. Add Restrict (hide) or Exclude (never used in answers) policies by team or department, resolve conflicts, preview what one person can access.

  • Works
    Single sign-on

    Add an OIDC or SAML connection, verify the domain with a DNS record, require SSO. New, not yet committed.

  • Partly works
    AI settings

    Every setting saves, but only "weekly digest to managers" changes anything. Strictness, pause answers, stale-source warning, route to owner and pause during checks are stored and ignored.

  • Works
    Audit log

    Search, date range, category filters, expand for details.

  • Server only
    Export the audit log, export workspace data, analytics

    The server supports CSV audit export, a full workspace export and an analytics overview. No screens.

  • Partly works
    Workspace setup checklist

    Connect a source, choose content, create a project, invite your team. Step 5, "Assign a new hire", says it isn't in the app yet.

  • Server only
    Grant special responsibilities

    Domain owner, mentor, path curator, bounty sponsor and others. Without a screen, nobody in production can verify knowledge items or resolve conflicts.

  • Server only
    Add guests to projects, give one person access to one source, see and revoke pending invites

    No screens.

  • Server only
    Rewards catalog, points adjustments, anomaly review

    Later phase. No screens.

What's missing for admins

  1. AI settings that actually change Context Bot's behaviour.
  2. Connectors beyond Google Drive.
  3. Directory sync (SCIM).
  4. A screen to grant domain owner and other responsibilities. Several knowledge features depend on it.
  5. Screens for guest project access, pending invites, exports and analytics.
  6. Step 5 of workspace setup.
  7. Fix the workspace export so it leaves out check-in notes. It currently includes them, breaking the "admins don't see check-ins" rule.
  8. A People item in the admin navigation, if admins should browse the directory.
OW
Role · owner

Owner

The person who created the workspace, plus anyone an owner promotes. Not in the spec. Owners see the same admin app, with a few extra powers that protect the workspace itself.

Nav: same as AdminData scope: whole workspace, no onboarding data

Features

  • Works
    Everything an admin can do

    See the Admin section.

  • Works
    Create a workspace

    Company name and address. The creator becomes Owner.

  • Works
    Make someone an Owner, or remove ownership

    Only owners can. The last owner can't be removed.

  • Works
    Can always sign in when SSO is required

    So a broken SSO setup can't lock the workspace out.

  • Server only
    Schedule or cancel workspace deletion

    No screen.

  • Server only
    Run performance review cycles

    Part of a later add-on. No screens.

What's missing for owners

  1. A screen to delete the workspace, with its grace period.
  2. The Owner role in the spec, so the team agrees on what only owners may do.

Open decisions and what the app does today

The ten open questions from section 8 of the role access spec, with the behaviour the code has settled on so far.

#QuestionWhat the app does today
1Can Admin see onboarding progress?No. All People Ops pages refuse admins. The workspace export is the exception and should be fixed.
2Who changes probation to full-time?The manager recommends and HR decides. Matches the prototype.
3Can a manager edit or remove team members?No. The server lets a manager invite a member into their own team, but there's no screen for it.
4Can HR add, edit and remove people and change roles?HR creates onboarding records, which send the invite, and can change a new hire's manager. Only admins change roles.
5Does HR get an audit log page?No.
6Do HR and Admin get their own learning roadmap?No. Only managers have "My learning".
7Can employees publish knowledge directly?No. Proposed answers go to the Review queue.
8Manager scope: direct reports or the whole tree?Direct reports by default. A workspace setting adds indirect reports, but only some pages honour it.
9One role per user, or several?One. An HR person can't also be a manager.
10Hide the manager Review queue for the demo?Still visible. It's no longer empty: it now holds bot answers proposed for the Hub, not only contributions.
